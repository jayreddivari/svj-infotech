About SVJ Infotech Engineering

SVJ Infotech Engineering provides high-performance structural steel detailing and design solutions for commercial and industrial construction projects.
We exist to bring discipline, precision, and execution clarity to every stage of the steel lifecycle — from modeling and detailing to fabrication and erection.

Our work is defined by constructability-first thinking.

Every drawing is developed with a deep understanding of fabrication processes, connection integrity, erection sequencing, and site coordination.


By aligning engineering intent with real-world execution, we help our partners reduce rework, minimize RFIs, and protect project margins.

We are execution-focused steel detailing partners for U.S. fabricators who need clarity, coordination, and reliability.

SVJ Infotech Engineering stands for reliability under pressure, precision without compromise, and partnerships built on performance.
