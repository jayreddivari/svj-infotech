Welcome to SVJ Infotech Engineering

SVJ Infotech Engineering, an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, was founded in 2024 by Mr. Siva Sodum, a seasoned expert with over 12 years of experience in Design and Detailed Engineering. Under his visionary leadership and relentless dedication, the company has experienced remarkable growth. This impressive growth continues as we scale new heights in our industry.

Our expertise spans a wide array of services, including:

  • BIM Services
  • Steel Detailing
  • Civil & Structural Design
  • Plant Engineering
  • Precast Detailing
  • Rebar Detailing

At SVJ Infotech Engineering, we are committed to delivering world-class engineering solutions, driving innovation, and ensuring the highest standards in every project we undertake. Join us in shaping the future of engineering.
