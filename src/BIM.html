Building information Modelling (BIM)

In Building information Modelling (BIM) we provide following services such as:

3D Modeling

Preparing precise, timely, and intelligent 3D model from design stage to as-built stage

Clash Coordination

Automated clash detection for multi-trade and inter-discipline to early identify the conflicts between different systems (e.g., plumbing and electrical) before construction begins, minimizing costly rework.

2D Drawing

Creating precise 2D drawings from the 3D model from design stage to as-built stage.

Quantity take-off

Extract model-based quantity for each trade and services for Bidding to Billing progression.

4D Scheduling

Integrate construction timelines into your models to visualize and manage project progression.

5D cost estimate

Incorporate cost data into your BIM models for real-time budget analysis and forecasting.

6D Facility management

Utilize BIM data for efficient lifecycle management, enhancing maintenance and operational workflows.
