About us

At SVJ Infotech Engineering, we approach steel detailing with an execution-first mindset. Our focus goes beyond modeling — we develop fabrication-ready and erection-ready drawings that align with real-world site conditions and project timelines. With U.S.-based coordination and responsive communication, we work closely with fabricators and contractors to minimize RFIs, reduce rework, and ensure smoother project execution.
We don't just deliver drawings, we deliver clarity, accountability, and confidence.

Our mission is to simplify complex challenges, enabling you to focus on what matters most – your success.

Let us be your trusted steel detailing partner — supporting you from concept to fabrication to successful erection. We are execution-focused steel detailing partners for U.S. fabricators who need clarity, coordination, and reliability.

Services

Structural Steel Detailing


We deliver precision-driven 3D modeling and detailing for beams, columns, bracing systems, trusses, and complex structural assemblies. Our detailing process focuses on constructability, coordination, and clarity — ensuring that every component aligns with design intent and real-world execution requirements.
